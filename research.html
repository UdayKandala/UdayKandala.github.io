Bio Engineering Research Policy

I am pursuing a PhD by Publication, building a six-paper research programme around Glyph Perturbation Cardinality (GPC), a raster-domain text steganography framework I originated. Information is hidden by perturbing individual pixels within rendered glyphs at the raster level, rather than in the vector or linguistic domain.

GPC Series

  • Paper 1, Glyph Perturbation Cardinality (GPC), Framework for Raster Domain Text Steganography (published)
  • Paper 2, Learned Pixel Selection using MLP and Graph Transformer via REINFORCE Learning
  • Paper 3, Capacity, Color Extension, Robustness and Steganalysis of GPC
  • Paper 4, GPC for Multimodal Data Transmission
  • Paper 5, Cross Language GPC using Zero Shot Learning
  • Paper 6, Optimized Cover Text Generation through LLMs and Learned MLP Selector
